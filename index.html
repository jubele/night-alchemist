Night Alchemist

A pixel-art potion shop for Android · Nočný alchymista

The Master is gone, and his workshop is yours. By day, customers describe what ails them and you work out which potion will help. By night, you equip hunters with oils and powders and send them into the graveyard, the marshes and the Wolf Forest – their log arrives line by line.

  • Brew from a recipe book that grows with your renown
  • Grow herbs in the cellar greenhouse, shop at the market
  • Send hunters on real-time expeditions and decide when things go wrong
  • Plays offline, in English and Slovak

The game is in closed beta on Google Play.
